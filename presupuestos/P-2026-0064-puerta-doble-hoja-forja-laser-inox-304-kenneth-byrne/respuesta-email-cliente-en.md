### 1. Recommended Material and Anti-Corrosion Protection for Menorca
In an island environment like Menorca, traditional mild steel or carbon-steel wrought iron inevitably develops rust over time, especially along welds, sharp edges, and decorative crevices. 

To eliminate this issue while achieving the exact classic ornamental aesthetic you are looking for, we propose manufacturing the entire gate in **AISI 304 Stainless Steel** (or marine-grade AISI 316), featuring:

- **Decorative ornamental elements CNC laser-cut from solid 10 mm stainless steel plate:** The top cresting with fleur-de-lis finial, arched scrolls, upper curls, lower ring frieze, and base volutes will all be precision laser-cut from solid 10 mm stainless steel. This delivers the substantial thickness, visual depth, and solid presence of traditional wrought iron with zero risk of flaking rust or structural degradation.
- **Structural frame & infill bars:** Tubular perimeter frames (40×40×2 mm) curved to the arched profile in our workshop, internal arched framing (40×20×2 mm), 12 solid square infill bars (14×14 mm), and mounting jambs—all assembled using high-grade TIG welding, followed by thorough chemical pickling and passivation.
- **Stainless steel hardware:** 6 heavy-duty adjustable stainless steel hinges (3 per leaf, designed for heavy loads), an exterior stainless steel mortise lock with a Euro-profile cylinder and 3 keys, matching handles, bottom drop bolt, ground stop socket, and heavy-duty chemical anchors with A4 stainless steel threaded studs.
- **High-durability two-component black exterior finish:** Surface degreasing and dedicated mechanical/chemical etching of the stainless steel to ensure optimum adhesion, followed by a **specialized epoxy wash primer for stainless steel** and **two coats of premium two-component aliphatic polyurethane enamel in black (RAL 9005)**. This provides a sleek, durable, UV- and salt-spray-resistant finish (available in satin, matt, or fine-textured iron finish).

*(Technical note: If your property is located directly on the seafront or exposed to severe wave spray/saline mist, we will evaluate during our site visit whether upgrading to **Marine-Grade AISI 316 Stainless Steel** is advisable.)*
